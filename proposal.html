Search Disciplines

The Search Trifecta: SEO vs. AEO vs. GEO

You noted that you want someone who knows what AEO and GEO mean without looking them up. Here is how we engineer all three into every single client site:

Traditional SEO

Search Engine Optimization

Dominating organic blue links & Google Local 3-Pack Map listings.

  • Service-City Silos: Discrete landing pages for high-intent submarkets (e.g., /roof-replacement-holland-mi/) rather than dilute catch-all pages.
  • Core Web Vitals Perfection: Sub-1.2s Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), 0 Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), and pristine mobile viewport scaling.
  • Technical Crawl Foundation: Dynamic XML sitemaps, strictly self-referencing canonicals, semantic HTML5 header hierarchy, and robot crawl budget efficiency.
  • GBP Synchronization: Mirroring exact primary and secondary service categories from Google Business Profile into on-page service silos.
Target Platforms: Google Organic SERPs, Google Maps / Local Pack, Bing Web.
AEO Native

Answer Engine Optimization

Getting picked as the immediate direct answer in Google AI Overviews & Featured Snippets.

  • Direct-Answer Architecture: Placing definitive 45–60 word answer summaries immediately under `H2` questions (costs, timeframes, warranty specifics).
  • Structured JSON-LD Feeds: Deep implementation of FAQPage, HowTo, and Service schemas that Google's algorithm can ingest without re-parsing raw layout.
  • Conversational Query Alignment: Structuring pages around real speech patterns ("who handles fallen tree removal near me on Sunday").
  • Zero-Click Win Strategy: Providing enough direct value to claim snippet position #0 while providing an irresistible CTA hook for full quote calculation.
Target Platforms: Google AI Overviews (SGE), Featured Snippets, Voice Assistants (Siri / Google Assistant).
GEO Pioneer

Generative Engine Optimization

Ensuring Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and Claude recommend and cite your contractor.

  • Information Gain & Fact Tuples: Ingesting verified local stats, permit rules, and material specs that LLMs recognize as high-entropy, authoritative sources.
  • Entity Disambiguation: Complete schema linkage using @id, Wikidata topics, and authoritative sameAs links (state licensing boards, BBB, Chamber of Commerce).
  • Comparison & Matrix Syntheses: Building structured tables (e.g., Architectural vs. 3-Tab Shingles in Michigan Freeze-Thaw Cycles) that LLMs preferentially grab as quotation sources.
  • Co-Citation Anchoring: Aligning digital footprint consistency so AI models form high probabilistic association between [Service + City] and the client.
Target Platforms: ChatGPT Search, Perplexity Pro, Claude Artifacts, Microsoft Copilot.
